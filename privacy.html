Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Who we are

Poultry Queen is a YouTube channel and a small publishing tool used by the channel owner to prepare and upload educational videos about poultry farming. This page explains what data the tool and this website handle.

This website

This website is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not run analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not ask visitors for any personal information. Like any website host, GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses according to GitHub's Privacy Statement.

The Poultry Queen app and Google user data

The Poultry Queen app is a private tool that connects only to the channel owner's own Google account and YouTube channel. With the owner's explicit permission it can:

  • upload videos to the Poultry Queen YouTube channel (permission "Manage your YouTube videos"), and
  • read basic channel information and public statistics of the owner's own videos, such as view counts (permission "View your YouTube account").

What we do with that data

  • View counts are used only to decide which video topics to make next.
  • The app's sign-in token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer. It is not sent to any third party.
  • We do not collect, store, sell, share or transfer data about YouTube viewers, subscribers or other users.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, and no human reads it other than the channel owner.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

The channel owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

Our content is aimed at adult farmers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. For questions, contact us through the Poultry Queen channel on YouTube (@PoultryQueen-q3m).
